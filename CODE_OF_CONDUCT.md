# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in the xdm project.

Participation includes issues, pull requests, code review, discussions, and
other communication made on behalf of the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- respect differing opinions and levels of experience;
- provide actionable technical feedback;
- accept responsibility for mistakes and corrections;
- protect private and security-sensitive information;
- focus discussions on the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination;
- insulting, derogatory, or unnecessarily hostile comments;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- personal or political attacks;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly sharing credentials, private messages, or security-sensitive data;
- sustained disruption of project discussions or contribution workflows.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and history of a violation, maintainers may issue a
private correction, a warning, a temporary participation restriction, or a
permanent ban from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented when appropriate, and
made with regard for the privacy and safety of affected people.

## Reporting

Report Code of Conduct violations privately to:

```text
willyz@outlook.com.br
```

Use the subject:

```text
[xdm conduct] Incident summary
```

Include relevant links, dates, context, and supporting material. Reports will be
reviewed as privately and fairly as possible.

Do not use public issues to report harassment or disclose another person's
private information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is officially
representing the xdm project in public spaces.
